## 2. Creative direction

The spot opens on paper and ink: a quiet grid, the Arabic wordmark, then a dark
vault panel that slides in from the right. Olive carries structure and trust;
coral marks the single point of action. The futuristic element is restrained —
a thin countdown arc and a hairline scan across a QR grid — never neon overload.

No stock photography, no generic padlock clip art, no stock-video silhouettes.
All frames are built from vector shapes so the ad stays crisp at every ratio and
cannot imply a security guarantee the app does not make.

## 4. Platform and format plan

- **Website:** 16:9 hero loop on the software page and the launch blog post;
  1:1 still key art for the software card.
- **GitHub release:** 1:1 animated preview plus a still of the lockup.
- **Studio social channels:** 9:16 cut for short-form feeds, 1:1 cut for square
  placements, 16:9 cut for video players.
- **Every export:** Arabic and English versions, captions burned in and supplied
  separately as SRT, mandatory disclosure visible in the final two seconds.

## 5. Accessibility and technical rules

- Burned-in captions must meet the same contrast rules as the site: paper/ink
  on light frames, void/white on dark frames; coral only for large accents.
- Provide a reduced-motion cut with no scan sweep and a static countdown arc.
- No autoplay audio in website embeds; mute by default and start on gesture.
- All assets are produced locally; the ad must never add an external font,
  script, CDN, analytics or tracking request to the studio website.

## 6. Do not change / do not invent

- No Hosin/Hussein — do not add, credit, or imply any fourth developer.
- No security-audit claims, audit badges, "audited", "verified secure", or
  equivalent wording. The app is not audited; it is in active development.
- No cloud-sync claims, "sync everywhere", "backed up to the cloud", or any
  suggestion of a remote service. There is no cloud sync.
- No real credentials, passwords, recovery phrases, or personal secrets in any
  visual, mock-up, caption, caption file, or test asset. Use abstract blocks.
- Contact must use the official studio email only: official5a1fstudios@gmail.com.
- Amin's personal email appears only on his team profile; never in ad copy,
  credits, watermarks, or asset metadata.
- Do not invent biometric unlock, encryption algorithms, cross-device sync,

- Do not add phone numbers, postal addresses, or school names to any asset or
  campaign copy.
